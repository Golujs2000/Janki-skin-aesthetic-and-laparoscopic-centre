## 📌 Google Business Profile Character Limits Reference Guide

| Field in Google Business Profile | Hard Limit | Best Practice / Recommended | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Business Name** | No fixed cap | Exact Registered Name Only | Avoid keyword stuffing to prevent profile suspension |
| **Business Description (About)** | **750 Characters** | **700 – 745 Characters** | **First 250 characters** appear before the "Read More" fold |
| **Service Name** | **120 Characters** | **30 – 60 Characters** | High-intent bilingual service title with exact keywords |
| **Service Description** | **300 Characters** *(Strict!)* | **240 – 295 Characters** | Google rejects any description > 300 characters |
| **Google Posts (Updates/Offers)** | **1500 Characters** | **300 – 600 Characters** | Optimal readability with clear Call-To-Action (CTA) button |

## 🛠️ 8. Step-by-Step Implementation Guide for Google Business Profile

1. **Log in:** Go to [Google Business Profile Manager](https://business.google.com) using your verified Google Account.
2. **Edit Services:**
   * Click **Edit Services** > Select your primary category (e.g., *Medical Clinic* or *Surgical Center*).
   * Click **+ Add more services** > Create custom services using the exact names from Section 3.
   * Paste each corresponding description into the **Service Description** box. Notice all 18 descriptions are between **248 and 267 characters** (well within Google's 300-char limit).
3. **Update Business Description:**
   * Go to **Edit Profile** > **About** > **Description**.
   * Paste the 731-character master description from Section 2.
4. **Publish Weekly Posts:**
   * Go to **Add Update** > choose **Add update** or **Add offer**.
   * Copy Post 1, Post 2, or Post 3 from Section 4, attach an attractive photo or clinic poster, and link the CTA button directly to the helpline phone number.
5. **Populate Q&A:**
   * On Google Maps, search for *Janki Skin Aesthetic & Laparoscopic Centre Forbesganj*.
   * Under the **Questions & Answers** section, post the 4 pre-seeded questions and immediately reply from the clinic's verified business profile.
