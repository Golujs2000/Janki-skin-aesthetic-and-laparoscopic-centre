About Our Centre

Compassionate Care, Advanced Technology & Clinical Precision

Janki Skin Aesthetic & Laparoscopic Centre (जानकी स्किन एस्थेटिक एवं लैप्रोस्कोपिक सेंटर) is Forbesganj’s premier multi-speciality clinical facility dedicated to solving complex dermatological conditions and delivering modern minimally invasive laparoscopic surgical care.

Conveniently located on Referral Hospital Road, Forbesganj, our centre bridges the gap between metropolitan surgical excellence and local accessibility. Patients no longer need to travel to Patna, Siliguri, or Delhi for advanced laparoscopic surgeries or specialized aesthetic dermatology procedures like GFC hair regrowth, glutathione therapy, and chemical peeling.

Why Choose Laparoscopic Keyhole Surgery?

Compare modern minimally invasive laparoscopic procedures conducted by Dr. S. Sagar with traditional open surgical techniques.

Feature / Parameter ✨ Laparoscopic Surgery (Janki Centre) Traditional Open Surgery
Incision & Scarring 3 to 4 tiny keyholes (0.5 to 1 cm) • Virtually invisible scar Large 10 to 15 cm cut with permanent prominent scar
Post-Op Pain & Discomfort Minimal pain; mild painkiller requirements Severe post-op pain requiring heavy narcotic injections
Hospital Stay & Discharge Same day or 24 to 48 hours discharge 5 to 8 days prolonged hospital admission
Return to Routine Life Normal light activities in 3 to 7 days 4 to 6 weeks extended bed rest
Risk of Wound Infection & Hernia Extremely low (< 0.5%) Higher risk of wound dehiscence & incisional hernia
Blood Loss Virtually bloodless; magnified high-definition camera view Significant blood loss during tissue incision

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about consultation schedules, laparoscopic surgeries, recovery periods, and aesthetic therapies.

Dr. Rashmi Ray (MBBS, MD Dermatology) is available for consultation every Wednesday (हर बुधवार) from 1:00 PM to 7:00 PM, and on Saturday & Sunday (शनिवार एवं रविवार) from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Advance booking is recommended to avoid waiting times.

Because laparoscopic cholecystectomy utilizes 3 to 4 tiny incisions (0.5 to 1 cm) without cutting abdominal muscles, patients typically mobilize and start taking oral fluids within hours. Most patients are discharged within 24 to 48 hours and resume light work within 5 to 7 days.

GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate) is the advanced evolution of PRP. In GFC, growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, EGF, IGF-1) are extracted directly from the blood's platelets into a sterile acellular solution. It delivers significantly higher biological activity, negligible pain, zero RBC contamination, and superior hair follicle regeneration.

No, chemical peels performed by a certified dermatologist are not painful; patients only feel a mild, temporary tingling sensation. Depending on whether you choose a gentle glow peel or deep pigment peel, minor micro-flaking occurs for 2 to 4 days, after which smooth, radiant, and renewed skin appears.
